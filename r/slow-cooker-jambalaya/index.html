Slow cooker chicken and sausage jambalaya

8 portions · Prep about 12 min in the morning, 10 min at dinner · Cook 7 to 8 hours on low · 6 to 7 qt slow cooker, rice cooker · about 45g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Chicken thighs raised to 3 lb (recipe uses 2 thighs) for 40g+ protein; vegetables and tomatoes 1.5x.
  • Rice cooked in the rice cooker instead of in the slow cooker (more reliable at this size), so less broth.
  • Pantry Cajun seasoning instead of measuring oregano, paprika, cayenne and pepper; mild smoked sausage, no shrimp.
  • Your frozen celery and onion; dried garlic; parsley and green onion garnish skipped.

Ingredients

  • About 2 cups frozen chopped celery (yours)
  • About 1 1/2 cups frozen chopped onion (yours)
  • 2 green bell peppers, diced
  • 1 tsp dried minced garlic
  • 3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, whole
  • (1) 14 oz smoked sausage (mild), sliced
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp Cajun seasoning (use 1 Tbsp for milder)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • (1) 28 oz + (1) 14.5 oz can diced tomatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • About 3 cups rice, uncooked

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Morning, by 9:00 am: put the celery, onion, peppers and garlic in the slow cooker. Lay the chicken thighs and sausage on top, sprinkle with the Cajun seasoning and thyme, and pour the tomatoes and broth over. Cook on low 7 to 8 hours.
