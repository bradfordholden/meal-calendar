Butter chicken with spinach over rice

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 35 min · Large deep skillet or Dutch oven · about 40g+ per adult portion, about 3g sugar

Our changes
  • 3 lb chicken instead of 2 and the sauce scaled 1.5x, so an adult portion hits 40g+ protein.
  • Baby spinach stirred in so it's a complete meal.
  • Mustard seeds left out.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast, cut in 3/4 inch chunks
  • 1 1/2 tbsp butter and 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 4 to 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 1/2 tsp each: chili powder, ground coriander, curry powder
  • 3/4 of a 6 oz can tomato paste
  • 1 jar (4 oz) red curry paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 to 2 cans (14 oz) coconut milk (use both for a saucier dish)
  • 10 oz baby spinach
  • To serve: rice, cilantro

Steps

  1. Start the rice.
  2. Melt the butter with the oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes, then add the ginger and garlic for a minute.
  3. Stir in the garam masala, chili powder, coriander and curry powder and toast for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken, tomato paste, curry paste, salt and pepper and stir until the chicken is coated.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk, bring to a boil, then turn down and simmer uncovered about 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and the sauce thickens.
  6. Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until wilted. Serve over rice with cilantro.

Adapted from Feel Good Foodie: Easy Butter Chicken.
