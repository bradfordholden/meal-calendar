Butter chicken with spinach over rice

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 35 min · Large deep skillet or Dutch oven · about 40g+ per adult portion, about 3g sugar

Our changes
  • 3 lb chicken instead of 2 and the sauce scaled 1.5x, so an adult portion hits 40g+ protein.
  • Baby spinach stirred in so it's a complete meal.
  • Mustard seeds left out.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast, cut in 3/4 inch chunks
  • 1 1/2 tbsp butter and 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 4 to 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 1/2 tsp each: chili powder, ground coriander, curry powder
  • (3/4) 6 oz can tomato paste
  • (1) 4 oz jar red curry paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • (1 1/2 to 2) 14 oz cans coconut milk (use both for a saucier dish)
  • 10 oz baby spinach
  • To serve: rice, cilantro

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Start the rice.

    • Rice
  2. 2

    Melt the butter with the oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes, then add the ginger and garlic for a minute.

    • 1 1/2 tbsp butter
    • 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
    • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
    • 4 to 5 garlic cloves, minced
  3. 3

    Stir in the garam masala, chili powder, coriander and curry powder and toast for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.

    • 1 1/2 tbsp garam masala
    • 1 1/2 tsp chili powder
    • 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
    • 1 1/2 tsp curry powder
  4. 4

    Add the chicken, tomato paste, curry paste, salt and pepper and stir until the chicken is coated.

    • 3 lb chicken breast, in 3/4 inch chunks
    • (3/4) 6 oz can tomato paste
    • (1) 4 oz jar red curry paste
    • 1 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  5. 5

    Pour in the coconut milk, bring to a boil, then turn down and simmer uncovered about 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and the sauce thickens.

    • (1 1/2 to 2) 14 oz cans coconut milk (both for a saucier dish)
  6. 6

    Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until wilted. Serve over rice with cilantro.

    • 10 oz baby spinach
    • Cilantro
