One-pot chicken broccoli ziti

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook about 40 min (our estimate about 50 total; recipe says 30) · Large Dutch oven (5.5 qt+) · about 45g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Doubled, with 2.5 lb chicken (instead of 2) for 40g+ protein.
  • Protein penne instead of whole wheat ziti.
  • Your frozen broccoli and frozen onion; jarred garlic; no red pepper flakes (kids); fresh parsley skipped.
  • Canned artichoke hearts left out (Brad, 2026-11-14).

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breast or tenders, cut in 1/2 inch strips
  • 6 Tbsp butter, divided
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, divided
  • About 1 1/2 cups frozen chopped onion (yours)
  • 2 Tbsp jarred minced garlic
  • 4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • (2) 14 oz cans or 28 oz low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 cups milk, plus more as needed
  • 12 oz protein penne or ziti
  • About 6 cups frozen broccoli (yours)
  • Juice of 2 lemons (about 1/4 cup)
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Heat 2 Tbsp butter and the oil in a big Dutch oven over medium-high. Cook the chicken in 2 batches with half the salt and pepper, about 5 min each, until golden. Set aside, covered.

    • 2.5 lb chicken, in strips
    • 2 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp butter, olive oil
  2. 2

    Turn to medium. Add the rest of the butter and the onion; cook 3 min. Add garlic, Italian seasoning and the rest of the salt; cook 30 seconds.

    • 1 1/2 cups frozen onion
    • 2 Tbsp jarred garlic
    • 4 tsp Italian seasoning
  3. 3

    Stir in the broth and milk and bring to a boil. Add the pasta and boil 5 min, stirring often.

    • 28 oz chicken broth
    • 3 cups milk
    • 12 oz protein penne
  4. 4

    Stir in the frozen broccoli and keep boiling 6 to 8 min until the pasta is al dente. Add a splash of milk if it gets dry. Meanwhile juice the lemons.

    • about 6 cups frozen broccoli
  5. 5

    Stir in the lemon juice, Parmesan and chicken. Season and serve with extra Parmesan.

    • 2 lemons, juiced
    • 1/2 cup Parmesan

Adapted from Well Plated: Chicken Broccoli Ziti.
