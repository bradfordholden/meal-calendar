Chicken noodle soup

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min (about 35 total) · Large soup pot (8 qt) · about 42g per adult portion, about 4g sugar

Our changes
  • Double batch with 3 lb raw chicken simmered in the broth (the original uses 2 cups cooked chicken), so an adult portion hits 40g+ protein.
  • Your frozen carrot and celery instead of fresh.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast (the leftover chicken, thawed), in 1/2 inch pieces
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 yellow onions, diced
  • (1) quart bag frozen chopped carrot and celery (yours)
  • 2 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 2 tsp flour
  • Salt and pepper
  • (3) 32 oz cartons low-sodium chicken broth
  • (1) 12 oz bag wide egg noodles
  • 3 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat and cook the onions until they start to soften, about 4 minutes. Meanwhile cut the chicken into 1/2 inch pieces.

    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 2 yellow onions, diced
    • 3 lb chicken breast
  2. 2

    Add the frozen carrot and celery and cook a few minutes until thawed. Stir in the poultry seasoning, flour, salt and pepper for a minute.

    • (1) quart bag frozen carrot and celery
    • 2 tsp poultry seasoning
    • 2 tsp flour
    • salt, pepper
  3. 3

    Add the broth and the chicken, bring to a boil, and simmer 8 minutes.

    • (3) 32 oz cartons low-sodium chicken broth
  4. 4

    Stir in the noodles and simmer about 7 minutes more, until tender and the chicken is cooked through. Stir in the parsley.

    • (1) 12 oz bag wide egg noodles
    • 3 tbsp parsley, chopped
  5. 5

    For lunches, the noodles soak up broth overnight; add a splash of broth or water when reheating.

    • Nothing new
