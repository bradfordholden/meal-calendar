Sheet pan chicken and green beans with lemon feta

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min · 2 sheet pans, 1 bowl · about 41g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Scaled about 3x for 8 portions at 40g+ protein; 2 sheet pans.
  • Skinless thighs so it's quick and not greasy.
  • Table salt amount halved from the kosher-salt amount.

Ingredients

  • 4 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 lb fresh green beans, trimmed
  • 1 cup olive oil, divided (1/3 cup for the pans, 2/3 cup for the topping)
  • 1 head garlic, cloves peeled
  • 3 lemons, zested and juiced, divided
  • 2 tbsp salt (less if using table salt: 1 tbsp)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp each oregano, smoked paprika and cumin
  • Black pepper
  • (2) 6 oz containers crumbled feta
  • 1 bunch fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 package fresh dill, chopped (about 1/4 cup)

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 425 F. Split the chicken and green beans between 2 sheet pans and toss with the oil, garlic, half the lemon juice, salt, oregano, smoked paprika, cumin and pepper. Spread in one layer.

    • 4 lb chicken thighs
    • 2 lb green beans
    • 1/3 cup olive oil
    • 1 head garlic cloves
    • half lemon juice
    • 1 tbsp salt
    • 1 1/2 tbsp each oregano, smoked paprika, cumin
    • black pepper
  2. 2

    Bake 20 to 25 min, swapping the pans halfway, until the chicken reaches 165 F.

    • Nothing new
  3. 3

    Meanwhile stir the feta, parsley, dill, the rest of the oil, the lemon zest, the rest of the juice and a pinch of salt and pepper in a bowl. Spoon over the chicken and beans.

    • (2) 6 oz containers crumbled feta
    • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
    • about 1/4 cup dill, chopped
    • 2/3 cup olive oil
    • 3 lemons zest + remaining juice
