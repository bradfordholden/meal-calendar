Sheet-pan chicken fajitas

8 portions · Prep 20 min · Cook 25 min · 2 rimmed sheet pans · about 40g+ per adult portion

Our changes
  • Doubled, with 3 lb chicken, for 8 portions.
  • Greek yogurt, cheese and avocado added as toppings.
  • Leftovers become rice bowls for lunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut in 1/2 inch strips
  • 4 bell peppers, any color, thinly sliced
  • 2 red onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • Spice blend: 4 tsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • 16 to 20 corn tortillas (6 inch)
  • 1 cup cilantro leaves
  • 4 limes, cut in wedges
  • To serve: Greek yogurt, shredded Mexican cheese, sliced avocado

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 400°F. Line two rimmed sheet pans with foil.

    • Nothing new
  2. 2

    Mix the spice blend in a big bowl. Add the chicken, peppers, onions and oil and toss until everything is coated.

    • 4 tsp chili powder
    • 2 tsp cumin
    • 2 tsp smoked paprika
    • 1 tsp garlic powder
    • 1 tsp onion powder
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp pepper
    • 1/4 tsp cayenne
    • 3 lb chicken breast, in 1/2 inch strips
    • 4 bell peppers, thinly sliced
    • 2 red onions, thinly sliced
    • 4 tbsp olive oil
  3. 3

    Spread over the two pans in a single layer. Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through, tossing and swapping the pans' racks halfway.

    • Nothing new
  4. 4

    While it roasts, warm the tortillas in a hot dry skillet until lightly browned.

    • 16 to 20 corn tortillas (6 inch)
  5. 5

    Fill tortillas and top with cilantro, a squeeze of lime, yogurt, cheese and avocado.

    • 1 cup cilantro leaves
    • 4 limes, in wedges
    • Greek yogurt
    • shredded Mexican cheese
    • sliced avocado
  6. 6

    Lunch: pack leftover chicken and peppers over rice for 3 bowls.

    • Rice, for 3 lunch bowls
