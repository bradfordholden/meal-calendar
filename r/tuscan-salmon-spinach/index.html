Creamy Tuscan salmon with spinach, rice and roasted green beans

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · 2 ovens, 4 sheet pans (2 salmon, 2 green beans) + large skillet · about 43g per adult portion, about 2g sugar

Our changes
  • 3.5 lb salmon instead of 1.5 lb and the sauce doubled, for 8 portions at 40g+ protein.
  • Salmon baked on sheet pans instead of pan-seared in batches, so a triple-size batch still takes about 30 minutes.
  • One garlic & herb shaker instead of separate pepper and garlic powder.
  • Green beans roasted in the second oven on the side, instead of steamed.

Ingredients

  • 3.5 lb salmon fillet, cut in 8 pieces
  • 1 tsp salt and a good shake of garlic & herb seasoning
  • 2 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp butter
  • 2/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped
  • 1/2 cup green onion, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup finely shredded parmesan
  • 4 cups (about 5 oz) baby spinach
  • To serve: rice, (2) 12 oz bags frozen cut green beans, roasted with 2 tbsp olive oil and salt

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Heat one oven to 425°F for the green beans and the other to 400°F for the salmon. Start the rice cooker (or use microwave rice). Spread the frozen green beans (no need to thaw) on 2 sheet pans, toss with the oil and salt, and roast 20 to 25 minutes, stirring halfway, until browned in spots.

    • Rice
    • (2) 12 oz bags frozen cut green beans
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 1/2 tsp salt
  2. 2

    Lay the salmon on 2 oiled sheet pans and season with the salt and garlic & herb seasoning. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until 145°F in the thickest part.

    • 3.5 lb salmon, in 8 pieces
    • 1 tsp salt
    • garlic & herb seasoning, a good shake
    • olive oil, for the pans
  3. 3

    Meanwhile melt the butter with the oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the sun-dried tomatoes, green onion and garlic about a minute, until fragrant.

    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 2/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
    • 1/2 cup green onion, chopped
    • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  4. 4

    Stir in the cream and parmesan, bring to a light boil, then simmer a minute or two until slightly thick.

    • 2 cups heavy cream
    • 1/2 cup finely shredded parmesan
  5. 5

    Stir in the spinach a few handfuls at a time until wilted. Season to taste.

    • 4 cups (about 5 oz) baby spinach
  6. 6

    Spoon the sauce over the salmon and serve with rice and the roasted green beans.

    • Nothing new
