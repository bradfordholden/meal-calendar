Protein pasta with Rao's and ground beef, roasted broccoli

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 boxes protein pasta
  • 2.5 lb ground beef
  • 2 jars (24 oz) Rao's marinara
  • 2 lb broccoli crowns, cut in florets
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the broccoli with oil, salt and pepper and roast about 20 minutes, until browned at the edges.
  2. Boil the pasta in salted water per the box.
  3. Meanwhile brown the beef in a large pot, breaking it up. Drain if needed, add the Rao's and simmer until the pasta is ready.
  4. Toss the drained pasta with the sauce and serve with the broccoli.
