Crock pot beef stew

8 portions · Prep 30 min in the morning, 15 min at the end · Cook 8 to 9 hours on low · 7 qt slow cooker (a full 6 qt works if packed), large skillet · about 42g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Beef raised to 3 1/2 lb and the rest scaled 1.5x for 8 portions at 40g+ protein.
  • Garlic powder swapped for dried minced garlic from the pantry.
  • On low (8 to 9 h) so it can start in the morning.

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 lb beef chuck stew meat, 1 inch chunks
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 3/4 tsp dried minced garlic
  • 4 1/2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 yellow onions, chopped
  • 6 cups beef broth, divided
  • 4 1/2 cups diced peeled potatoes (about 2 1/2 lb)
  • 3 cups carrots, 1 inch pieces (about 1 lb)
  • 1 1/2 cups celery, 1 inch pieces (about 4 ribs)
  • 1 1/2 cups V8 vegetable juice
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
  • 3/4 tsp dried thyme
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp water
  • 1 cup frozen peas

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Toss the beef with the flour, salt, pepper and dried garlic.

    • 3 1/2 lb beef stew meat
    • 1/3 cup flour
    • 1 1/2 tsp salt
    • 3/4 tsp black pepper
    • 3/4 tsp dried minced garlic
  2. 2

    Brown the beef in batches in 3 tbsp oil over medium-high, about 2 min per side, and move it into the slow cooker.

    • 3 tbsp olive oil
  3. 3

    Turn the heat to medium, add the rest of the oil and cook the onions until soft. Add them to the slow cooker.

    • 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
    • 2 yellow onions, chopped
  4. 4

    Pour 1 1/2 cups broth into the skillet and scrape up the brown bits; pour it into the slow cooker.

    • 1 1/2 cups beef broth
  5. 5

    Add the rest of the broth, the potatoes, carrots, celery, V8, Worcestershire, rosemary and thyme. Cook on low 8 to 9 hours.

    • 4 1/2 cups beef broth
    • about 2 1/2 lb potatoes, peeled and diced
    • about 1 lb carrots, 1 inch pieces
    • about 4 ribs celery, 1 inch pieces
    • 1 1/2 cups V8
    • 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    • 1 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
    • 3/4 tsp dried thyme
  6. 6

    20 minutes before dinner, stir the cornstarch into the water, stir it in a little at a time until thick, add the peas, cover and cook 10 to 15 minutes. Salt and pepper to taste.

    • 3 tbsp + 3 tbsp cornstarch + water
    • 1 cup frozen peas
