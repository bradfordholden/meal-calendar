Teriyaki chicken stir fry over rice

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · large deep skillet or wok, rice cooker · about 44g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Chicken 3 1/2 lb for 8 portions at 40g+ protein.
  • Store-bought teriyaki sauce instead of the homemade sauce (Brad, 2026-11-07).
  • Broccoli as the mixed vegetable.

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, bite size pieces
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 red bell peppers, strips
  • 2 lb broccoli crowns, small florets
  • (2) 8 oz cans sliced water chestnuts, drained
  • About 1 1/2 cups store-bought teriyaki sauce (2 bottles, about 10 oz each)
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped, divided
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • Rice, about 4 cups uncooked

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Start the rice.

    • about 4 cups rice, uncooked
  2. 2

    Heat 1 tbsp oil in the big skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken in 2 batches, 4 to 6 min until cooked through. Move to a plate.

    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • 3 1/2 lb chicken breast, bite size
  3. 3

    Add the last 1 tbsp oil, then the peppers and broccoli; cook 4 to 5 min until crisp-tender.

    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • 2 red bell peppers, strips
    • 2 lb broccoli florets
  4. 4

    Add the water chestnuts and chicken, pour in the teriyaki sauce, toss and simmer 1 to 2 min. Stir in half the green onions; top with the rest and the sesame seeds. Serve over rice.

    • (2) 8 oz cans water chestnuts, drained
    • about 1 1/2 cups teriyaki sauce
    • 1 bunch green onions
    • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
