Slow-cooker turkey chili (double batch)

8 portions · Prep 25 min · Cook 4 to 5 h on low · 6 qt slow cooker · about 45 to 50g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Double batch with 3 lb turkey instead of 2, so an adult portion hits 40g+ protein.
  • Greek yogurt instead of sour cream.
  • Needs a 6 qt slow cooker (the original fits a 4 qt).

Ingredients

  • 3 lb lean ground turkey
  • 2 green bell peppers, finely chopped
  • 2 small red onions, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 2 cans (16 oz) kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 3 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth (most of a 32 oz carton)
  • 3 1/2 cups frozen corn, thawed
  • 2 cans (6 oz) tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • To serve: plain Greek yogurt, shredded Mexican cheese, cilantro

Steps

  1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the turkey with the peppers and onions until the turkey is no longer pink, breaking it up as it cooks. This is a lot of meat, so do it in two batches if the pan is crowded. Add the garlic for the last minute, then drain.
  2. Move everything to the slow cooker and stir in the tomatoes, beans, broth, corn, tomato paste and spices.
  3. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 5 hours.
  4. Serve topped with Greek yogurt, cheese and cilantro. Leftovers keep 4 days in the fridge and freeze well.

Adapted from Taste of Home: Family-Pleasing Turkey Chili.
