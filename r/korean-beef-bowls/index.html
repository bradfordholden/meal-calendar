Korean ground beef rice bowls with steamed broccoli

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min (about 30 total) · Large skillet, rice cooker, microwave · about 40g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Scaled 3.5x for 8 portions at about 40g protein.
  • Brown sugar halved to keep sugar down (about 9g per portion).
  • Red pepper flakes left out for the kids; sriracha at the table instead.
  • Steam-in-bag broccoli on the side as the vegetable.

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 lb lean ground beef (90/10)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 head garlic (about 10 cloves), minced
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar (half the recipe's amount, scaled)
  • 3/4 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 bunch green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • (2) 12 oz bags frozen steam-in-bag broccoli
  • To serve: rice (about 4 cups uncooked)

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Start the rice. Whisk the brown sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil and ground ginger in a bowl.

    • about 4 cups rice, uncooked
    • 1/2 cup brown sugar
    • 3/4 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
    • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    • 1 tsp ground ginger
  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the garlic about 1 minute.

    • 1 tbsp avocado oil
    • 1 head garlic, minced
  3. 3

    Add the beef and cook 6 to 8 minutes, crumbling it, until browned. Drain the fat. (Do it in 2 batches if the skillet is crowded.)

    • 3 1/2 lb lean ground beef
  4. 4

    Stir in the sauce and most of the green onions and simmer 2 minutes. Meanwhile microwave the broccoli bags.

    • 1 bunch green onions, sliced
    • (2) 12 oz bags steam-in-bag broccoli
  5. 5

    Serve the beef over rice with broccoli on the side, topped with the rest of the green onions and the sesame seeds. Sriracha at the table for adults.

    • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Adapted from Damn Delicious: Korean Beef Bowl.
