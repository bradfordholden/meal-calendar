Slow cooker yellow chicken curry over rice

8 portions · Prep 20 min · Cook about 4 h on high · 6 qt slow cooker · about 44g per adult portion, about 8g sugar

Our changes
  • Double batch with 3 lb chicken instead of 1 lb, so an adult portion hits 40g+ protein.
  • Cayenne and turmeric left out (curry powder already has turmeric), so curry powder is the only spice and the kids will eat it; hot sauce at the table instead.
  • Salt halved because the chicken is salt-injected.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast (the leftover Market District chicken, thawed)
  • (2) 13.5 oz cans coconut milk (full fat)
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth or water
  • 1/4 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp yellow curry powder (or regular curry powder)
  • 1/2 tsp salt (half the original, since the chicken is salt-injected), black pepper
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes (yours), in 1/2 inch cubes
  • about 1 lb frozen chopped carrots
  • 2 yellow onions, diced
  • 2 red bell peppers, sliced thin
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  • To serve: rice, cilantro, lime wedges, hot sauce for the adults

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Whisk the coconut milk, broth, peanut butter, soy sauce, garlic and ginger in the slow cooker, then stir in the curry powder, salt and pepper.

    • 1/2 cup chicken broth or water
    • 1/4 cup creamy peanut butter
    • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
    • 6 garlic cloves, minced
    • 2 tbsp grated fresh ginger
    • 2 tbsp yellow curry powder
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • black pepper
  2. 2

    Add the chicken, then the sweet potatoes, carrots and onions, and stir so the liquid covers everything.

    • 3 lb chicken breast, thawed
    • 4 medium sweet potatoes, in 1/2 inch cubes
    • about 1 lb frozen chopped carrots
    • 2 yellow onions, diced
  3. 3

    Cover and cook on high about 4 hours (or low 6 to 7), until the chicken is tender and the sweet potatoes are soft.

    • Nothing new
  4. 4

    Lift out the chicken, shred it with two forks and stir it back in.

    • Nothing new
  5. 5

    Add the bell peppers and peas and cook on high 15 to 20 minutes more, until the peppers soften.

    • 2 red bell peppers, sliced thin
    • 2 cups frozen peas
  6. 6

    Start the rice while the peppers cook. Serve the curry over rice with cilantro and lime; adults can add hot sauce.

    • Rice
    • Cilantro and lime wedges
    • Hot sauce, for the adults
