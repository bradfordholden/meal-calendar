Slow cooker salsa chicken bowls with black beans and corn

8 portions · Prep 5 min in the morning, 5 min at dinner · Cook 6 to 8 hours on low · 6 qt slow cooker, rice cooker · about 42g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Scaled 3x for 8 portions at 40g+ protein.
  • Adobo swapped for salt; garlic powder swapped for dried minced garlic from the pantry.
  • Regular shredded cheddar.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • (2) 12 oz bags frozen corn
  • (2) 15 oz cans black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp dried minced garlic
  • (2) 16 oz jars mild chunky salsa
  • (1) 8 oz bag shredded cheddar
  • To serve: rice (about 4 cups uncooked), cilantro, avocado

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Morning: put the corn and black beans in the slow cooker.

    • (2) 12 oz bags frozen corn
    • (2) 15 oz cans black beans, rinsed and drained
  2. 2

    Season the chicken with salt, cumin and garlic, lay it on top and pour the salsa over. Cook on low 6 to 8 hours (set it to switch to warm after 7 hours if it can).

    • 3 lb chicken breast
    • 1 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
    • 1/2 tsp dried minced garlic
    • (2) 16 oz jars mild chunky salsa
  3. 3

    About 30 minutes before dinner, start the rice.

    • about 4 cups rice, uncooked
  4. 4

    Shred the chicken with two forks right in the pot, sprinkle the cheese on top, cover 5 minutes to melt. Serve over rice with a slotted spoon, with cilantro and avocado.

    • (1) 8 oz bag shredded cheddar
    • cilantro, avocado
