Sweet potato shepherd's pie with beef and corn

8 portions · Prep about 40 min · Cook 30 min bake (about 75 total, Sunday bulk meal) · Microwave, large skillet, 9x13 + 8x8 baking dishes · about 44g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Scaled 4x for 8 portions with 3.5 lb beef (recipe ratio would be 2 lb) for 40g+ protein.
  • Uses your frozen onion and frozen corn instead of fresh onion and canned corn.
  • The dash of allspice is left out; cinnamon and nutmeg are from the pantry.
  • About 26g sugar per serving, almost all from the sweet potatoes, corn and tomato sauce (no added sugar).

Ingredients

  • 4 large sweet potatoes (yours)
  • 3.5 lb lean ground beef (90/10)
  • About 1 cup frozen chopped onion (yours)
  • About 4 cups frozen corn (yours), thawed
  • (1) 15 oz can tomato sauce
  • 1/2 tsp each ground cinnamon and nutmeg
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking spray

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 350°F. Scrub and pierce the sweet potatoes and microwave them on a plate, 2 at a time, 10 to 12 min per batch, turning once, until soft.

    • 4 large sweet potatoes
  2. 2

    Meanwhile brown the beef with the onion in a large skillet in 2 batches, breaking it up, until no longer pink. Drain.

    • 3.5 lb lean ground beef
    • about 1 cup frozen chopped onion
  3. 3

    Stir in the corn, tomato sauce, cinnamon and nutmeg. Spread in a sprayed 9x13 and 8x8 dish.

    • about 4 cups frozen corn, thawed
    • 15 oz can tomato sauce
    • 1/2 tsp each cinnamon, nutmeg
    • cooking spray
  4. 4

    Scoop out the sweet potato flesh and mash with the butter, milk, salt and pepper. Spread over the beef.

    • 4 Tbsp butter
    • 1/4 cup milk
    • salt, pepper
  5. 5

    Bake uncovered 25 to 30 min until hot through.

    • Nothing new
