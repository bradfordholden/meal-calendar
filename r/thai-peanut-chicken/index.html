Slow cooker Thai peanut chicken with sweet potatoes over rice

8 portions · Prep 15 min · Cook about 3 1/2 to 4 h on high · 6 qt slow cooker · about 45g per adult portion

Our changes
  • Scaled to 8 portions with 3 lb chicken for 40g+ protein.
  • Sweet potatoes and peas added as the vegetables (both fit the dish); spinach left out.
  • Honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil and fish sauce left out to use what's on hand; lime covers the tang.
  • Red pepper flakes at the table instead of in the pot, for the kids.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast (thawed), in bite-size pieces
  • 2 yellow onions, diced
  • 2 red bell peppers, diced
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes, in 1/2 inch cubes
  • 1/2 tsp salt (the chicken is salt-injected), 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • (1) 13.5 oz can full-fat coconut milk
  • 3/4 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • To serve: rice, cilantro, red pepper flakes at the table for adults

Steps

Do thisYou'll need
  1. 1

    Put the chicken, onions, peppers and sweet potatoes in the slow cooker and season with the salt and pepper.

    • 3 lb chicken breast, bite-size pieces
    • 2 yellow onions, diced
    • 2 red bell peppers, diced
    • 4 medium sweet potatoes, 1/2 inch cubes
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  2. 2

    Whisk the coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce, garlic and ginger until smooth and pour it over everything. Stir so the sweet potatoes are coated.

    • (1) 13.5 oz can full-fat coconut milk
    • 3/4 cup creamy peanut butter
    • 3 tbsp soy sauce
    • 6 garlic cloves, minced
    • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  3. 3

    Cover and cook on high 3 1/2 to 4 hours (or low 5 to 6), until the chicken is cooked and the sweet potatoes are tender.

    • Nothing new
  4. 4

    Stir in the peas and lime juice and cook 10 more minutes. Start the rice meanwhile.

    • 2 cups frozen peas
    • 2 limes, juiced
    • rice
  5. 5

    Serve over rice with cilantro. Adults can add red pepper flakes.

    • cilantro, red pepper flakes
