Blackened salmon rice bowls

8 portions · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min

Ingredients

  • Costco blackened salmon, 8 portions
  • Rice, about 3 cups uncooked
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 2 x 5 oz arugula
  • Japanese BBQ sauce

Steps

  1. Start the rice.
  2. Cook the salmon per the package directions.
  3. Build bowls: rice, arugula, salmon, avocado, and a drizzle of Japanese BBQ sauce (go light, it's sweet).
