Thai yellow chicken curry over jasmine rice

8 portions · Prep 20 min · Cook 35 min · Large Dutch oven or deep pot · about 40g+ per adult portion

Our changes
  • Store-bought Thai yellow curry paste, not curry powder.
  • Double batch with 3 lb chicken for 8 portions at 40g+ protein.
  • Sweet potatoes and frozen carrots instead of Yukon golds and fresh carrots.
  • Sugar cut to 1 tbsp for the double batch.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken breast, in 1 inch cubes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 yellow onions, in 1 inch pieces
  • (1) 4 oz can Thai yellow curry paste (Maesri or Mae Ploy); use 3/4 of it for milder
  • 2 red bell peppers, in strips
  • about 1 lb frozen chopped carrots
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes, in 3/4 inch cubes
  • (4) 13.5 oz cans full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt
  • To serve: jasmine rice, lime wedges, cilantro

Steps
